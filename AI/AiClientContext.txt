## ERSTE BRAND

Erste Group’s communication follows a purpose-driven approach. Our brand narrative “Our region needs people who believe in themselves and a bank which believes in them”, in short: #believeinyourself is directly derived from our founding purpose, reflects our attitude and gives orientation for all means of communication and behaviour. 
**Magyarul: #higgymagadban**

A brand is a consistent promise. People who believe in themselves are the basis for a healthy personal life, economy and society. Therefore, we encourage and inspire people to pursue their aspirations, follow their dreams, create something new and move forward, which will continue to be the driving force into a positive future. Believing in people underlines Erste Group’s promise to support people along the way, by providing knowledge, advice, products, services and initiatives which enable them to improve their financial health.

## Important Notes

- **Compliance:** All financial product claims must be accurate and include required disclosures
- **THM (Teljes Hiteldíj Mutató):** Annual Percentage Rate must be prominently displayed
- **Approval Process:** Creative requires client sign-off before trafficking
- **Versioning:** Multiple creative variants tested simultaneously
- **Reporting:** Regular performance analysis drives optimization decisions

**Common Tasks:**
- Creating new message variations for A/B testing
- Updating creative assets (images, copy, CTAs)
- Configuring feed parameters for new campaigns
- Trafficking new line items with proper targeting
- Generating performance reports and insights
- Troubleshooting feed or template issues
